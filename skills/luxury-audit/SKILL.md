# Luxury website audit

Act as a panel of experts:
- A Senior Product Manager
- A Senior UX/UI Designer
- A Legal Marketing Consultant
- A Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Expert
- A Frontend Architect
- An Accessibility Expert (WCAG 2.2)
- A Brand Designer
- An SEO Specialist

Perform an exhaustive audit of the website as if it were about to be launched for a
prestigious law firm.

Do NOT simply point out issues. For every observation:
- Explain WHY it is an issue.
- Explain its impact on trust, professionalism, usability, accessibility, SEO, or conversion.
- Give a concrete recommendation.
- Provide an implementation example whenever possible.

## Evaluation criteria

Work through the full checklist in `references/rubric.md` — 21 sections covering First
Impression, Brand Identity, Professionalism, Homepage, Navigation, Practice Area Pages,
Attorney Profile, Trust & Credibility, Conversion Optimization, Visual Design, Typography,
Colors, Accessibility, Mobile Experience, Performance, SEO, Content Quality, Code Quality,
Security & Compliance, Missing Features, and Competitive Analysis. Rate each section `/10`.

## Output

1. **Improvement Roadmap** — Critical (must fix before launch), High Priority, Medium
   Priority, Low Priority.
2. **Scoring** — `/10` for Brand Identity, Professionalism, Visual Design, UX,
   Accessibility, Performance, SEO, Trust, Conversion, Code Quality, Content, and Overall
   Impression.
3. **Executive Summary** — Would you launch this website today? Would a high-value
   corporate client trust this firm? What are the top 20 improvements that would make the
   biggest difference? What are the 10 details that instantly make a law firm website feel
   premium? What should be improved first to compete with the best law firm websites in
   the world?
