# Profile — back office, point of sale, stock and accounting tool

**Extra personas:** Payments & Accounting Expert, Store Operations Lead (the cashier and the
manager who use it all day).

SEO is `N/A` behind the login. Brand is judged as clarity and trust, not luxury.

## P1. Selling at the till
Open the till, ring a sale, every payment method (cash, card, mobile money, credit), change
due, receipt, a sale over the stock, a sale offline then synced, close the till and its count.

## P2. Payments & debts
Each method recorded with amount, method, actor, store, time and reference; a declared
payment confirmed by someone else; partial payments reconcile to what is owed; a payment
retried twice is recorded once; cash sales counted as money received. Say whether payments
are only **recorded** (the money moves outside the app) or **collected** through a provider
(then audit the provider integration as in the e-commerce profile, P3).

## P3. Corrections
Cancel, return, refund: each has its permission, its stock effect, its money effect and its
journal entry; the figures after a correction still reconcile.

## P4. Stock & cost
Receipts, adjustments, transfers, negative stock, FIFO or average cost, margins in reports.

## P5. Reports & exports
Totals that match the sales and payments screens, filters (store, period, seller, method),
CSV export, the store and period named on the report.

## P6. Team & stores
Invitations, roles, store access, a member removed mid-session, several stores per owner.

## P7. Daily operations
Backups and a tested restore, what happens when the network drops, device registration,
time zones, printing, low-end phones.

## Executive-summary questions
- Can a shop run a full day on it: open, sell with every method, correct a mistake, close, report?
- Would the owner's figures match the cash in the drawer and the mobile-money statements?
