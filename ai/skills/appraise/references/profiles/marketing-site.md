# Profile — marketing site, landing page, portfolio, blog

**Extra personas:** SEO Specialist, CRO Expert, Brand Designer, Copywriter.

## P1. Brand & message
Is the value proposition clear in five seconds? Logo, palette, photography, tone, consistency.

## P2. Homepage & landing pages
Hero, headline, proof (logos, testimonials, numbers), sections order, CTA, footer.

## P3. Conversion
CTA placement and wording, forms and their friction, contact options, newsletter, analytics
events on the conversions that matter, spam protection on forms.

## P4. SEO
Titles, meta descriptions, one `h1` per page, structured data (`Organization`, `Article`,
`Product` as fitting), Open Graph and Twitter cards, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt,
`hreflang` for several languages, internal links, alt text, Core Web Vitals.

## P5. Content
Freshness, readability, blog or resources, duplicate pages, broken links.

## P6. Competitive analysis
Against three sites of the same market: what makes this one weaker, what stronger?

## Executive-summary questions
- Would a first-time visitor understand the offer and act?
- Which pages would rank, and which never will as they stand?
