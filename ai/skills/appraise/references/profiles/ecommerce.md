# Profile — e-commerce, online shop, marketplace

**Extra personas:** CRO Expert, Payments Expert, SEO Specialist, Brand Designer.

## P1. Catalog
Product pages: photos, price, stock, variants, description, reviews; search, filters, sorting.

## P2. Cart & checkout
Steps and friction, guest checkout, shipping cost shown early, promo codes, address forms.

## P3. Payments
Each payment method end to end in the provider's test mode: success, refusal, cancel,
timeout; webhooks verified and idempotent; the order state matches the provider's; refunds;
receipts and invoices; amounts and currency formatting.

## P4. Orders & after-sale
Order confirmation email, order tracking, returns and refunds, customer account.

## P5. Trust
Legal notice, terms of sale, returns policy, secure-payment cues, reviews, contact.

## P6. SEO
`Product` and `Offer` structured data, canonical URLs on variants, sitemap, category pages.

## Executive-summary questions
- Can a customer pay with every method offered, and get their money back?
- Where does the funnel lose people?
