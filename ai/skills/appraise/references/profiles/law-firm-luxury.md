# Profile — law firm, luxury brand, high-end professional services

**Extra personas:** Legal Marketing Consultant, CRO Expert, Brand Designer, SEO Specialist.

Audit as if the site launched tomorrow for a prestigious firm. Imagine a CEO, a judge or a
corporate executive visiting it.

## P1. Brand identity
Logo quality, palette, typography, consistency, icons, illustrations, photography,
whitespace, premium feeling, luxury perception. Would a client trust the firm on branding alone?

## P2. Professionalism
Credibility, seriousness, authority, confidence, tone of voice, overall polish.
List every detail that makes the site look amateur.

## P3. Homepage
Hero, headline, subheadline, CTA, trust indicators, statistics, testimonials, practice-area
preview, attorney introduction, contact information, footer. What is missing?

## P4. Practice-area pages
Each service page: problem, solution, benefits, process, FAQ, attorney expertise, case
examples, CTA, contact options, internal links, trust indicators.

## P5. Attorney profiles
Portrait, biography, education, bar admissions, experience, awards, certifications,
languages, publications, media, memberships, LinkedIn, contact CTA.

## P6. Trust & credibility
Testimonials, case results, awards, certifications, associations, press, publications,
years of experience, success metrics, affiliations, Google Reviews, office and attorney photos.

## P7. Conversion
CTA placement and wording, lead capture, contact forms, phone visibility, WhatsApp where
fitting, appointment booking, trust near the CTA, form friction, clicks to contact.

## P8. SEO
Titles, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, structured data (`LegalService`, `Attorney`),
Open Graph, canonical URLs, internal links, alt text, sitemap, robots.txt, local SEO.

## P9. Expected features
Online appointment scheduling, case evaluation form, live chat, FAQ, knowledge center, legal
blog, resource downloads, office locations and maps, languages, accessibility options.

## P10. Competitive analysis
Against the best law-firm sites: would it sit among premium firms? What makes it weaker, what stronger?

## Executive-summary questions
- Would a high-value corporate client trust this firm?
- Which 10 details would instantly make it feel premium?
- What to improve first to compete with the best law-firm sites in the world?
